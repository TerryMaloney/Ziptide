## 2. Locked role

> The Assembler Mk I accepts one recipe-approved industrial equipment frame in the asymmetrical square socket and one recipe-approved refined biological component in the round socket, closes a protected cradle, physically joins them with guarded tool arms, and returns one exact finished field item to the same cradle.

It does not:

- refine raw biology;
- process ore or salvage;
- accept raw seeds, raw organs, ore chunks, or finished equipment as ingredients;
- expose joining arms while player hands are inside;
- use the future catalyst in Mk I;
- create items through a magical beam or unexplained duplication;
- choose random output;
- own conveyor routing;
- override stronger dedicated output keepers.

## 3. Dominant machine and socket grammar

Primary player-facing zones:

1. **left industrial socket** — asymmetrical square/open frame recess;
2. **right biological socket** — round/family-keyed refined component recess;
3. **future catalyst location** — fully covered by solid bolted plate;
4. **protected assembly cradle** — same area receives finished output;
5. **guard** — transparent or heavy mesh, closes before tools move;
6. **commit lever** — physically unavailable until valid seating and guard closure;
7. **cancel/return control** — available before commit;
8. **guarded articulated arms** — visible but inaccessible during work;
9. **mechanical/analog state indicators** — support ready, locked, working, complete.

Square means industrial structure. Round means refined biological behavior. Triangle remains unavailable until taught and approved.

## 6. Industrial socket contract

GI-MSPEC-001 controls final geometry.

Required behavior:

- always left side in Mk I;
- open asymmetrical square recess;
- keyed edge/tooth receiver plus offset/chamfer stop;
- accepts one Equipment Frame Blank category;
- cannot accept solid plate, biological component, ore chunk, or finished tool;
- one-hand seating without fine finger alignment;
- enough frame remains visible to confirm category;
- valid seating uses depth, detent, haptic, mechanical indicator and audio;
- invalid orientation stops early and remains retrievable;
- no green-light-only meaning.

## 7. Biological socket contract

The biological input is refined and recipe-ready—not raw harvest.

Locked category traits:

- round dominant silhouette in Mk I;
- one coarse key preventing wrong orientation;
- broad grab surface;
- family anatomy remains visible after seating;
- no pinch zone;
- valid component can be retrieved before commit;
- raw organ/seed cannot seat;
- invalid component or wrong recipe pairing prevents commit.

### Unresolved carrier decision

The concept images show a round socket, but current biological inputs differ:

- Filter Membrane/cartridge-like insert;
- Grip Surface Insert;
- refined Conductive Vein package/channel insert.

Before implementation, choose one:

1. **universal round carrier ring** — every refined biological material is stabilized into a compatible carrier;
2. **family-specific round keyed adapters** — socket accepts a small set of recipe-authored insert carriers;
3. **output-frame-specific sub-cradles** — industrial frame brings the exact biological seat.

The choice must preserve child readability, recipe determinism, Quest simplicity, and visible family identity. No choice is authorized by concept art alone.

## 9. Guard and tool-arm interlock

Tool arms may move only when:

1. industrial component valid and fully seated;
2. biological component valid and fully seated;
3. pairing resolves to one declared recipe output;
4. guard fully closed;
5. guard latch mechanically locked;
6. no hand collider in protected volume;
7. service access closed;
8. commit lever deliberately operated.

Working state:

- arms remain behind guard;
- player hands outside;
- joining motion physically relates two seated components;
- no magical disappearance before contact;
- exact output appears only after atomic transaction completes;
- arms fully retract before guard unlocks;
- no dangerous pinch point.

## 12. State model

### Empty

- both sockets open;
- catalyst covered;
- guard open;
- commit unavailable;
- cancel inactive or returns nothing.

### Partial

- one valid component seated;
- second category remains visibly required;
- commit unavailable;
- seated component removable;
- spoken/caption may announce missing category once: `Add a refined biological component.` or `Add an Equipment Frame.`

### Valid pair / ready

- both components at final depth;
- recipe resolves deterministically;
- guard can close;
- mechanical ready indicator changes;
- commit remains unavailable until guard locks.

### Locked

- guard closed/latch engaged;
- hands clear;
- commit lever becomes available;
- cancel-before-commit returns both inputs only after safe guard release.

### Working

- commit transaction recorded;
- arms join components;
- guard stays locked;
- no input retrieval;
- state resolves atomically.

### Complete

- arms retract;
- output occupies cradle;
- guard unlocks;
- output can be collected;
- optional spoken/caption: `Invention ready.`

### Invalid / fault

- mismatched category/orientation/pair refuses commit;
- item remains retrievable;
- no consumption;
- non-color mechanical refusal plus help phrase;
- service fault cannot expose player to moving arms.

## 13. Accessibility contract

Working language:

- spoken name: `Assembler.`
- short function: `Combines a frame and plant material into equipment.`
- left empty socket: `Equipment Frame goes here.`
- right empty socket: `Refined biological material goes here.`
- partial state: `One more part is needed.`
- wrong category: `That part does not fit this socket.`
- invalid pair: `These parts do not make an invention yet.`
- ready: `Both parts are seated.`
- complete: `Invention ready.`

Icon grammar:

- industrial input: asymmetrical square/open frame;
- biological input: round family insert;
- future catalyst: hidden triangle;
- guard state: open/closed physical silhouette;
- commit: large lever only moves when ready;
- cancel: red physical return control with shape and label/caption support;
- working: arms moving behind guard;
- complete: finished compact belt silhouette in cradle.

Narration follows focus dwell/replay rules and never requires reading generated labels.

## 14. Comfort and safety

- no hand in cradle while arms can move;
- guard closure slow/readable and reverses before lock if obstructed;

- seated/child reach for sockets, guard, lever, cancel and output;
- no repetitive assembly pumping;
- no flash-heavy fabrication effect;

- no sharp protrusions at hand path.
