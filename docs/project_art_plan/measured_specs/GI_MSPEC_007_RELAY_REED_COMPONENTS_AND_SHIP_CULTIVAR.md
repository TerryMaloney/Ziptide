## 2. Locked family role

> Relay Reed is a Conduct-family organism that biologically routes low-voltage current through visible internal pathways and produces harvestable Conductive Vein plus controlled-storage Charge Nodules.

It is not:

- a lightning weapon;
- a magic battery tree;
- ordinary copper wire or bamboo;
- a universal power source;
- a high-voltage bypass;
- a constant neon light;
- assigned to a canonical world by this visual contract.

## 3. Locked visual grammar

### Seed

- palm-sized asymmetric split-cylinder puck;
- two offset contact ridges;
- keyed orientation readable by silhouette and touch;
- no generic round seed bag or battery cap.

### Living plant

- one shared root crown;
- six to eight graphite-brown segmented reeds in ship cultivar;
- copper biological pathways beneath smoky translucent sheath;
- limited forked two-prong contact tips near upper sections;
- upright clustered growth rather than tree canopy or tentacle mass;
- restrained violet-white pulse traveling along one active route only;
- no full-body constant glow.

### Conductive Vein

- three to five irregular segmented strands;
- embedded copper paths beneath smoky tissue;
- layered biological cut end;
- flexible but not rope-like or manufactured cable;
- restrained pulse only while current routes.

### Charge Nodule

- ceramic-white/smoky organic shell;
- one internal spiral;
- two asymmetrical contact ridges;
- short torn organic collar;
- natural specimen variation;
- no threaded base, standard battery cap, bulb fitting, or constant glow.

## 5. Seed and grow-socket contract

- asymmetric insulated socket repeats both offset ridges;
- seed cannot fully seat in wrong rotation;
- one coarse insertion motion, not precision threading;
- visible final depth and physical stop;
- mechanical snap/detent, short haptic, and audio confirmation;
- no electrical pulse before valid seating and growth state;
- wrong orientation gently stops and remains retrievable;
- inactive second plot remains covered in the introductory pair;
- exact planting UI/state governed by growing-system authority.

Working guidance phrase: `Turn the Relay Reed Seed until both ridges match.`

## 6. Ship cultivar layout contract

- compact below-waist cluster;
- six to eight reeds from one root crown;
- low ceramic insulated rack/support;
- no tree canopy or sprawling floor cables;
- forked tips near upper third;
- two or more reachable Charge Nodules only when ripe state is authored;
- one clearly removable Conductive Vein bundle near root crown or dedicated harvest point;
- one guarded low-voltage test terminal for functional demonstration;
- one analog lamp/gauge response;
- one active route at a time;
- no touchscreen-heavy management requirement;
- plant and rack remain within seated/child reach without requiring deep lean.

## 7. Harvest-component contract

### Conductive Vein harvest

- harvest point clearly distinct from living structural reeds;
- removal does not resemble cutting household wire;
- one bundle-sized output with visible biological end;
- remaining plant state communicates harvested/not-ready through anatomy and physical state;
- no constant dangerous current while being handled;
- active low-voltage test requires guarded contacts;
- exact yield/regrowth governed by progression data.

### Charge Nodule harvest

- ripe organ attached by short biological collar;
- collar/attachment visibly different from insulated contact ridges;
- player can grasp broad body without pinching a tiny stem;
- removal motion and force remain simple and safe;
- nodule does not spark in open air;
- guarded cradle required for discharge/test behavior;
- specimen variation preserved without changing socket category;
- exact storage/discharge role and recipes remain provisional.

## 8. Functional test contract

The nursery demonstration must show route and response, not electrical spectacle.

1. one forked tip seats against guarded low-voltage terminal;
2. terminal mechanically confirms contact;
3. one restrained violet-white pulse begins at root/route origin;
4. pulse travels through one visible copper path toward contact;
5. pulse remains contained inside plant/terminal path;
6. analog lamp or gauge responds;
7. no open-air arc, attack, blast, or target-hit effect;
8. test may be automatic at ripe/active state or deliberately triggered—activation remains unresolved;
9. reduced-flash setting must preserve function through gauge, mechanical state and sound.
