- mature wild form now reads as an upright reed cluster from one root crown rather than a tree canopy;

## 3. Known weakness accepted at concept stage

Charge Nodules remain the weakest visual element. Their oval ceramic/smoky forms may still read partly as manufactured bulbs. Preserve them only under these constraints:

- grown from a visible short organic collar;
- asymmetric contact ridges;
- one internal spiral;
- slight irregularity between nodules;
- no standard bulb base, battery cap, threaded fitting, or corporate indicator design;
- no constant emissive glow.

Component-sheet work must strengthen their biological cut/attachment anatomy before measured-spec approval.

## 4. Locked provisional family grammar

- **seed:** palm-sized asymmetric split-cylinder puck with two offset contact ridges;
- **body:** clustered graphite-brown segmented reeds;
- **routing anatomy:** copper-colored biological paths beneath smoky translucent sheath;
- **tips:** forked two-prong contact whiskers, not leaves;
- **storage organ:** ceramic/smoky Charge Nodule with one internal spiral and organic collar;
- **active state:** one restrained violet-white pulse moving along one route at a time;
- **growth habit:** upright root-crown colony with limited upper branching;
- **ship cultivation:** below-waist cluster on insulated ceramic rack;
- **harvest:** flexible Conductive Vein bundle plus detachable Charge Nodule.

## 5. Locked trait communication

> Conduct-family organisms route, bridge, store, or safely discharge small controlled electrical loads through visible biological pathways.

Trait understanding must come from physical cause and effect:

1. pulse begins at root/source;
2. pulse travels along one visible route;
3. contact tip reaches a low-voltage terminal;
4. analog device responds;
5. no combat arc, lightning attack, or universal hacking result occurs.
