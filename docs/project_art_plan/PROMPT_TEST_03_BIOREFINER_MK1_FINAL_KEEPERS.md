## 2. Why the machine body now closes

- normal loading uses a large asymmetric keyed tray rather than hand access to exposed rollers;
- input and lower output trays are visibly separate;
- chamber and latch read as guarded and mechanical;
- service-open view shows rollers with a disconnected power lead, separating maintenance from normal play;

- one large process lever and one return/cancel control dominate the intended interaction;
- compact patched salvage-metal body remains consistent with the ship, nursery, and invention station.

## 3. Locked role and interaction grammar

> One raw biological item enters a removable keyed tray, locks inside a guarded chamber with the correct process cassette, and exits as one durable recipe-ready biological material through a separate output tray.

Interaction order:

1. seat one raw biological item in the keyed tray;
2. slide the tray fully into the chamber;
3. close and mechanically lock the latch;
4. expose/enable one large process lever;
5. communicate ready, locked, working, and complete through lever/latch/gauge positions plus restrained light and sound;
6. deliver one refined item to the lower output tray;
7. cancel before commit returns the input;
8. interruption after commit resolves atomically;
9. no normal-play state exposes rollers, gears, heat surfaces, or cutting tools;
10. all controls remain reachable by seated adults and young children.
