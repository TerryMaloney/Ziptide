## §1 — The two failure modes this design must dodge

1. **The worn-out scavenger/steampunk look** (rust, pipes, chipped paint) — that's the Scrapper;
   we're leaving it on purpose. Cal keeps it as a distant/berth LOD + nostalgia, not the L3+ ride.
2. **The "seamless nothing"** — clean chrome or blank ceramic with no detail (that's the WARDEN's
   language, and it reads as EMPTY, not advanced). Terry's explicit no.

**The principle that beats both:** *advanced = density of INTENT + energy doing visible work,
pristine not grimy.* Four levers: **layered transparency** (you see it working) · **reconfigurable
geometry** (parts move/float) · **energy-as-structure** (hard-light, containment fields, glowing
channels) · **fine purposeful micro-detail** (fractal radiators, sensor lattices). Clean surfaces
EARN their emptiness only next to dense, intricate, glowing working elements.

## §2 — The three distinctive hooks (so it's OURS, not generic sci-fi chrome)

1. **Force-field-held floating hull segments** — the plates hover APART, held by visible light-
   fields, and reconfigure: folds tight/streamlined for flight, blooms open into a working rig
   at a site. "How is that even flying" is the target reaction. (No mechanical hinges = the
   anti-steampunk statement.)
2. **The frozen-current channel language** (the locked Architect syllabary/motif) glowing across
   a **semi-transparent iridescent hull** — **cyan = drive/signal energy, amber = Architect
   structure.** This is the through-line to the gate, the key, RILL, the coupler: the ship is
   visibly the same makers' work. It's what makes it non-derivative.
3. **The evolved drive-heart** — the coupler grown up: the artifact key seats deeper into a
   radiant core you can see pulsing through the transparent hull. The ship's soul, upgraded.

**Distinct from the Warden capital** (smooth pale ceramic + one blank eye): the MK2 is
iridescent + crystalline + covered in intricate glowing channel-work + floating segments +
grafted salvage gear. Never blank, never a single cyclopean eye. Different maker, different soul
(Architect = builders; Warden = immune system).

## §3 — THE CARRY-OVER RULE (Terry's ask + the character beat that saves it)

Cal **transplants her home's guts into the new body**: the external **grabber arm**, the
Scrapper's chunky **cockpit module** (real levers/toggles/floor-window), her **oil lantern**,
the **coupler**, the **bird trinket**, the tally/red-laces spirit — grafted onto the hyper-
advanced hull as **warm, human, salvaged islands.** This:
- keeps Cal's IDENTITY across the upgrade (continuity of home),
- keeps VR READABILITY (you still grab physical chunky controls, not sterile glass),
- **kills the "empty" failure mode by construction** (there's always warm human detail against
  the advanced hull),
- tells the story "she made it hers" — the reward feels earned, not gifted.
It's the **red-laces principle at ship scale.** Canon: the graft is deliberate and visible — a
salvage cockpit pod welded into an Architect frame, the arm on a jury-mount, the lantern hung
inside the glowing bridge.

## §4 — Origin canon (⚖ Terry chose Architect-derived)

rebuilds around). It is hyper-advanced BECAUSE it's the same civilization as the gate and the

## §5 — Skin-shop payoff (a reason this upgrade matters commercially)

An advanced modular energy-hull is a FAR better skin canvas than rust: skins vary **energy color
· alloy/material family · faction dress · channel patterns**, all cleanly. **Cal's grafted
personal gear stays CONSTANT across every skin** (her ship-scale identity anchor — you always
know it's Cal's ship no matter the skin). The MK2 is effectively the skin PLATFORM; the Scrapper
stays a fixed nostalgia hull. Design the base hull with skin-swap zones in mind (hull plates,
channel emissive, trim) vs. fixed zones (the grafted salvage islands).
