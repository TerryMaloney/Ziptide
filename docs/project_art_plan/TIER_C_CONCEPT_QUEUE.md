## §0 — Workflow + bookkeeping (same discipline as the music keepers)

- Generate ≥6 candidates per prompt; keep 1–2 per view. Iterate at the SILHOUETTE level first
  (a bad silhouette with great rendering is a rejected concept).
- Keepers land in `concepts/<asset_id>/` (repo or Terry's drive — his call) named
  `<asset_id>_<view>_v<N>.png`, with the winning prompt logged in `concepts/PROMPTS_LOG.md`.
- **The approval ceremony per asset:** Terry picks THE sheet → it's logged as canonical
  reference on the asset's row here → the asset becomes eligible for geometry (WC-5 intake).
- **The selection rubric (from the ship doc's six laws — applies to every asset below):**
  ① fill it black — nameable from the outline alone; ② one dominant feature, rest
  subordinate; ③ every detail claims a function; ④ reads at Quest draw distance AND at
  arm's length; ⑤ obeys the salvage-yard grammar (one builder's hands, even on bolt-ons);
  ⑥ a kid could draw it from memory after one look.

## §1 — LEAD BRIEF: THE SHIP (Cal's Toxic City Scavenger — exterior + interior)

**Canon (already locked — the prompts encode it, don't reinvent):** utilitarian blue-collar
salvage rig · asymmetrical, heavily recycled · one OVERSIZED patched engine (the dominant
feature) · external grabber arm (the hero part) · exposed heat sinks, oversized floodlights,
chipped hazard paint, mismatched plating · thrust logic (engines rearmost, mass toward nose,
cockpit forward/high with a real sightline, boarding door low on the flank) · cockpit = a
commercial transport CAB: panoramic view, roll cage, FLOOR WINDOWS, chunky levers and
physical toggles · it is HOME (quarters with bunks, trophy shelves, the contract ledger) ·
palette: used-future industrial neutrals + chipped hazard amber + ONE crest-cyan accent
(the artifact coupler's glow — the ship's heart) + warm practical lamplight inside.

**Paste-ready prompts (any image generator; append "no text, no watermark, no people"):**

- **S1 · Silhouette sheet (run this FIRST — it's the decision):** *"Design sheet of 6
  different small spaceship silhouettes, solid black on white, side view. Blue-collar salvage
  tug: stubby asymmetric hull, one oversized engine block at the rear, external articulated
  grabber arm, forward raised cockpit cab, low boarding hatch, rugged landing legs.
  Utilitarian, used-future, industrial. Each silhouette distinct. Flat vector style."*
- **S2 · Hero exterior (winning silhouette described in place of [SILHOUETTE]):** *"Concept
  art of a small salvage spaceship, 3/4 front view, landed on a wet dock at pre-dawn.
  [SILHOUETTE]. Heavily recycled asymmetric hull, mismatched welded plating, chipped amber
  hazard paint, exposed heat sinks and pipes, oversized floodlights, one huge patched engine,
  external grabber arm folded along the flank. A single cyan-glowing coupler socket near the
  heart of the ship. Used-future 70s sci-fi film aesthetic, Prospect movie feel, grounded
  industrial realism, muted colors, soft overcast light."*
- **S3 · Rear/thrust view:** same body text, *"rear 3/4 view emphasizing one enormous
  patched engine nozzle and maneuvering thrusters at the corners, radiators glowing faint
  amber."*
- **S4 · Cockpit interior (VR-critical — this is where the player LIVES):** *"Interior
  concept art of a small salvage spaceship cockpit, wide panoramic windshield, reinforced
  roll cage, GLASS FLOOR PANELS showing the dock below, one worn pilot seat, chunky
  mechanical levers and physical toggle switches, small analog gauges, warm practical
  lamplight, taped notes and worn grips, used-future 70s sci-fi, lived-in, cozy-industrial,
  first-person eye level."*
- **S5 · Quarters (the bunk wall — family profiles live here):** *"Interior concept art of a
  tiny spaceship crew quarters, a wall of 4 compact bunks with personal name tags and small
  shelves of salvaged trinkets, warm string lights, one round porthole, patched blankets,
  lived-in and warm, used-future industrial, cozy."*
- **S6 · The beauty shot (doubles as title-menu reference):** *"A small battered salvage
  spaceship moored at a dock over calm dark water at pre-dawn, one warm lantern glowing on
  the dock, giant ringed planet low on the horizon, monumental glowing letters across the
  water, quiet, melancholy, beautiful, used-future 70s sci-fi cinema."*

**Variation axes to explore across takes:** engine placement (rear-center vs offset —
asymmetry is canon, find the GOOD asymmetry) · cab high-forward vs embedded · grabber arm
side · how much hull is "one ship" vs visibly welded salvage (aim ~70/30).
