# PROMPT TEST 02 VERDICT — W002 CISTERN GRIP FAMILY

**Status:** prompt experiment PASSED. Visual direction is promising but remains non-canon until Terry explicitly approves a keeper. No runtime, recipe, WorldSpec, scene, or gameplay authorization follows from this verdict.

**Source experiment:** `docs/project_art_plan/PROMPT_TEST_02_CISTERN_GRIP_FAMILY.md`

## 1. Overall verdict

The structured prompt method successfully produced a second biological family that:

- remains visually distinct from the Moss Filter at thumbnail size;
- reads as gripping/anchoring through anatomy rather than color or labels;
- belongs naturally in W002’s monumental wet industrial cistern;
- preserves ZIPTIDE’s grounded used-future and child-readable design language;
- transfers coherently from species anatomy to harvest outputs and ship cultivation.

The experiment therefore validates the playbook’s core method: structured brief first, then delta-only refinement.

## 2. Candidate assessment

### A. Species lifecycle sheet — REFINE, near-keeper

**Strengths**

- asymmetric clamp seed is memorable and palm-readable;
- dark olive braided tendons and pale grip pads establish a distinct family grammar;
- mature wild state spreads laterally across a wall rather than becoming a tree;
- detachable Grip Pads, Grip Fiber, wild scale, and ship-cultivar scale are all visible;
- amber load nodules communicate tension without becoming quest glow.

**Failures to correct**

- sprout and juvenile drift toward ordinary leafy plant anatomy;
- lifecycle continuity is weaker between seed, juvenile, and mature forms than in the Filter keeper;
- mature wall form becomes visually tangled enough that individual load-bearing tendons are hard to parse;
- some pale pads read as leaves or cloth tabs instead of gripping organs;
- generated labels are reference-only and not trusted as canon.

**Playbook score:** 17/20.

### B. W002 discovery scene — ENVIRONMENT KEEPER / PLANT READABILITY REFINE

**Strengths**

- excellent Dry Cistern identity: monumental arches, deep shaft, wet masonry, pumps, catwalks, pale daylight shaft, and human machinery layered over older construction;
- cinematic while still resembling a traversable route;
- restrained lighting and scale feel unmistakably ZIPTIDE;
- Gripvine placement on a wet vertical pump column is ecologically logical.

**Failures to correct**

- plant blends too easily into ropes, cables, and industrial clutter;
- pale pads can read as tied cloth or maintenance tags;
- amber compression nodes are nearly invisible at gameplay distance;
- the image does not yet clearly show the plant bearing a specific load or preventing a component from slipping;
- harvest opportunity is not readable enough without prior explanation.

**Verdict:** preserve the environment composition; refine only plant hierarchy and function readability.

### C. Resource output sheet — GRIP PAD KEEPER / GRIP FIBER REFINE

**Strengths**

- Grip Pad is immediately understandable, palm-scaled, and demonstrably useful;
- underside microtexture and compressed amber marks communicate function;
- glove, wet slab, and temporary clamp demonstrations are production-useful;
- biological rim and mechanical clamp show how organic output can enter the invention economy.

**Failures to correct**

- Grip Fiber still reads too much like ordinary rope or hemp;
- fiber needs stronger shared anatomy with the living tendons: segmented sheath, pale connective strips, and restrained amber pressure nodes;
- generated text is not an interaction authority.

**Verdict:** Grip Pad is the strongest proposed keeper candidate in this batch.

### D. Departure/return pair — STATE-PAIR KEEPER, anatomy refine

**Strengths**

- same station, camera, lighting, and environment are preserved;
- growth is the dominant state change;
- keyed clamp seed and trellis make the departure setup understandable;
- return state is compact and ship-appropriate;
- warm amber, worn metal, gauges, and physical controls maintain ZIPTIDE’s home-base tone.

**Failures to correct**

- return plant still reads partly as a generic vine bundle;
- grip pads need to visibly attach to trellis bars under tension rather than float like leaves;
- one harvestable Grip Fiber bundle should be visibly ready to remove;
- seed and mature form need one more shared two-lobed clamp motif.

**Verdict:** strong keeper composition; refine anatomy only.

## 3. Family grammar learned

The Grip family’s working visual grammar is:

- asymmetric two-lobed clamp seed;
- dark olive braided living tendons;
- pale rubberized grip pads with microhook/suction-scale undersides;
- restrained amber compression nodes only where load is carried;
- lateral surface-spreading growth;
- harvest outputs: detachable Grip Pad and biological Grip Fiber;
- ship cultivar trained against a trellis;
- no honeycomb filtering anatomy and no cyan-dominant state language.

This is sufficiently distinct from the Filter family while remaining inside ZIPTIDE’s wider visual canon.

## 4. Prompt phrases that worked

- `palm-sized asymmetric clamp seed puck`;
- `dark olive braided root-cables`;
- `broad pale rubberized grip pads`;
- `restrained amber compression nodules`;
- `spreading two to three metres across a vertical wet wall`;
- `monumental underground waterworks cathedral`;
- `preserve the exact environment between panels so growth is the only major change`;
- explicit bans on Filter honeycomb anatomy, cyan quest glow, ordinary ivy, and fantasy cave crystals.

## 5. Drift patterns learned

- models may translate `plant` back into ordinary leaves during early stages;
- braided biological fiber easily becomes ordinary rope unless living anatomy is repeated explicitly;
- pale grip pads can become leaves, cloth tags, or mushrooms unless attachment/load behavior is shown;
- wall-spreading plants can become unreadable tangles without a small number of named primary tendons;
- environmental integration can hide the target when its palette resembles cables and wet machinery.

## 6. Recommended delta-only refinements

### Species sheet

> Preserve the exact production-sheet layout, asymmetric clamp seed, dark olive braided tendons, pale grip pads, amber load nodules, wall-spreading mature form, harvest components, and ship-cultivar inset. Change only three things: replace the juvenile’s ordinary leaves with two-lobed clamp shoots that visibly develop from the seed anatomy; reduce the mature form to six clearly readable primary tendons before they branch; and make every pale Grip Pad visibly press flat against stone or metal rather than resemble a leaf or cloth tab. Preserve the same scales and palette. Do not add flowers, honeycomb membranes, cyan glow, tentacle-monster anatomy, or another species. No text or watermark.

### Discovery scene

> Preserve the exact Dry Cistern environment, camera, player position, bridge, shaft of pale daylight, wet pump column, and grounded dark palette. Change only the Gripvine readability: organize it into four or five clearly separated braided primary tendons, show each pale pad compressed flat against the rusted column, increase the restrained amber pressure nodes just enough to read at gameplay distance, and show the plant visibly holding one loose pipe brace or maintenance panel in place against gravity. Add one reachable detachable Grip Pad near hand height. Do not add arrows, bright quest glow, ordinary ivy, cloth tags, mushrooms, or extra creatures. Keep the same cinematic playable-route composition. No text or watermark.

### Grip Fiber

> Preserve the Grip Pad, resource-sheet composition, gloved-hand scale, wet-surface demonstration, and salvage clamp examples. Change only the Grip Fiber so it no longer resembles ordinary rope: show a living multi-strand tendon with dark olive segmented sheath, thin pale rubberized connective bands, and tiny restrained amber pressure nodes at bends. Include one cut end revealing layered biological fibers. Do not add hemp texture, synthetic nylon rope, cyan glow, tentacles, or unrelated plant anatomy. Keep the same neutral production-sheet format. No text or watermark.

### Departure/return pair

> Preserve the exact two-panel ship nursery, camera, amber lighting, keyed seed socket, trellis, gauges, worn metal, and plant scale. Change only the return plant: make the same two-lobed clamp anatomy from the seed repeat at each branch junction, show four pale Grip Pads visibly flattened against trellis bars under tension, and add one small coiled Grip Fiber bundle ready to harvest. Do not redesign the station, add another species, use cyan-dominant glow, or make the plant larger. No text or watermark.
