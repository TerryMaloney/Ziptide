## 3. Machine role recommendation

Keep the BioRefiner's role narrow:

> Stabilize and prepare raw biological outputs into durable recipe-ready biological materials.

It does **not** assemble final equipment. `Assembler` remains the owner of combining refined biological material with industrial frames/components.

The BioRefiner may support different inputs through one guarded chamber plus a small number of visible process cassettes or modes. It should not visually imply an unexplained universal grinder that performs every manufacturing operation.

## 4. Required v1 interaction grammar

1. player places one raw biological item into a large removable loading tray or keyed cradle;
2. tray slides into a fully guarded chamber;
3. door mechanically locks before motion begins;
4. one large physical process control becomes available;
5. analog/shape feedback shows working and complete states;
6. refined output arrives in a separate reachable tray;
7. cancel before commit returns the raw input; interruption after commit resolves atomically;
8. no hand can reach blades, rollers, heat surfaces, or gears;
9. controls remain understandable through shape and movement, with text/captions secondary;
10. machine height and reach support seated adults and young children.
