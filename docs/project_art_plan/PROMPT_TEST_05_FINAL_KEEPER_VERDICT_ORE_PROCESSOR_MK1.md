## 2. Why the Ore Processor closes

- broad, low, heavy floor-mounted silhouette is distinct from BioRefiner and Assembler;
- removable ore tray, guarded compression chamber, heavy crossbar, high-resistance handle, and separate lower output drawer establish a clear manual process;
- dangerous compression mechanisms are enclosed during normal play;
- open-service view separates maintenance from player interaction;
- adult and seated/child reach references are present;
- pressure gauges, handle position, crossbar position, and restrained amber working state communicate force without relying only on text or color;
- worn dark steel, structural ribs, hydraulic lines, wear plates, grease, and dust establish the industrial branch of ZIPTIDE's ship language;
- input and output use different openings and different silhouettes;
- machine remains manual and local rather than becoming an unexplained automated factory or conveyor system.

## 3. Locked role

> The Ore Processor accepts one raw mineral or salvage-metal input in a removable keyed tray, locks it inside a guarded compression chamber with one installed material cassette, and returns one standardized recipe-ready industrial component through a separate lower drawer.

The Ore Processor does not refine biology, join final equipment, route conveyors, or create finished weapons.

## 4. Locked v1 interaction grammar

1. raw mineral/salvage input is placed into one asymmetric removable tray outside the machine;
2. tray slides fully into the guarded chamber;
3. heavy mechanical crossbar locks across the chamber;
4. selected processing cassette is visible through a stable physical identifier;
5. large high-resistance handle becomes available only when locked;
6. ready, locked, working, and complete states use crossbar, handle, gauge, drawer, restrained light, haptics, and sound;
7. separate lower drawer presents one standardized industrial output;
8. cancel before commit returns the raw input;
9. interruption after commit resolves atomically;
10. dangerous compression, heat, and moving structures remain inaccessible in normal play;
11. tray, crossbar, handle, cassette access, and output drawer remain reachable by seated adults and young children.

## 5. Industrial output vocabulary

The output sheet establishes four useful silhouettes:

- **Structural Plate Blank:** square reinforced body plate;
- **Equipment Frame Blank:** asymmetrical square/open frame for Assembler industrial sockets;
- **Conductive Strip:** flat flexible powered-tool material;
- **Dense Anchor Bar:** short heavy high-load structural stock.

The sheet is a vocabulary and transformation reference. Raw inputs, cassette icons, labels, and exact recipes remain provisional. W000/W001 should expose only the minimum output required by the first invention; the larger vocabulary enters later through progression.

## 6. Equipment Frame Blank keeper

The separate prop sheet is the strongest cross-machine contract:

- asymmetrical square silhouette;
- unmistakable keyed/toothed edge;
- reinforced corners and repairable fasteners;
- one-hand VR scale reference;
- visible placement in Ore Processor output drawer;
- visible acceptance by Assembler square socket;
- no biological anatomy.

Exact dimensions shown by the generator are not locked. Measured-spec work must verify mass, one-hand handling, socket depth, child reach, Quest collider simplicity, and belt/inventory representation.

The key geometry—not generated text—defines compatibility.

## 7. Composite keeper law

The Ore Processor keeper is composite:

## 8. Prompt-pipeline learning

This experiment validates three additional controls:

1. define machine identity through force and interaction, not only surface styling;
2. show the same component at source machine, hand scale, and destination socket;
3. separate vocabulary breadth from early-game exposure.

The most valuable result is the Equipment Frame Blank integration sheet: concept prompts can document a physical contract across two machines before implementation, provided dimensions and IDs remain unapproved until measured-spec work.
