## 3. Locked role

> The Cistern Grip Clamp temporarily braces one small repair component, cable coupling, tool, or loose industrial panel against wet stone or metal so the player can work with the other hand.

It is not a grappling device, traversal launcher, weapon, remote tether, permanent construction tool, or combat restraint.

## 4. Locked v1 interaction grammar

1. player retrieves one compact one-slot clamp from the belt;
2. keyed jaw is placed against an eligible small object and supporting surface;
3. pale biological pad visibly compresses flat against the target;
4. player pulls one large tension lever;
5. mechanical locked state, jaw position, amber pressure nodes, haptic pulse, and sound confirm load;
6. object remains braced while the player's other hand performs the repair/action;
7. release control unloads tension before removal;
8. invalid surfaces or excessive loads refuse to lock without consuming or damaging the item;
9. device cannot be fired, thrown as a special weapon, or used at long range;
10. controls and target placement remain usable by seated adults and young children.

2. active biological state must appear through deformation/load, not decorative glow;

4. an accessibility card should be evaluated separately from equipment beauty art.
