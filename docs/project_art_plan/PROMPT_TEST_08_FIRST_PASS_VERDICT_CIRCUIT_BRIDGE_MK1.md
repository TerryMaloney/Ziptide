## 8. Prompt-pipeline learning

This failure adds a binding equipment check:

> Utility intent does not survive if silhouette, grip, pose, contacts, or effects borrow weapon grammar.

For every non-weapon held utility, evaluate separately:

- **silhouette:** barrel, muzzle, trigger, pistol grip, stock, aiming rail;
- **pose:** one-handed pointing or two-handed weapon stance;
- **effect:** projectile, arc, beam, muzzle flash, target hit;
- **contact direction:** forward launch versus local docking;
- **persistence:** must the tool remain held, or can it dock and free the hands?

Negative words such as `not a gun` are insufficient when the requested geometry still describes a gun. The prompt must prescribe a non-weapon interaction and silhouette positively.
