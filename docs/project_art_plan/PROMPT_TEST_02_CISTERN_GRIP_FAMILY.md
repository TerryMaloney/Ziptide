# PROMPT TEST 02 — W002 CISTERN GRIP FAMILY

**Status:** concept-prompt experiment only. Not approved gameplay canon, not an asset keeper, and not runtime authorization.

**Purpose:** test whether `CONCEPT_ART_PROMPT_PLAYBOOK.md` can produce a second plant family that remains unmistakably ZIPTIDE while communicating a different function and avoiding visual copying of the Moss Filter family.

---

## 1. Experiment hypothesis

A structured brief should produce a coherent **Grip** family whose function is readable from silhouette and anatomy:

- it anchors to wet stone and industrial surfaces;
- it produces detachable gripping pads and tough braided fiber;
- it belongs naturally in W002’s Dry Cistern;
- it shares ZIPTIDE’s used-future, grounded, child-readable design law;
- it does not reuse the Filter family’s honeycomb membranes, round filter organs, pale leaf anatomy, or cyan-dominant activity.

---

## 2. Structured brief

**Working family name:** Cistern Gripvine

**Trait verb:** grips / sticks / anchors

**World context:** W002, The Dry Cistern—monumental underground waterworks, wet black stone, rusted pumps, ore conveyors, deep shafts, catwalks, one pale daylight beam, old Architect masonry beneath crude human industry.

**Ecological function:** grows through cracks near constant seepage and anchors itself against floods, vibration, and slick vertical surfaces.

**Gameplay-facing output:** detachable Grip Pads and braided Grip Fiber. Their exact recipe or equipment use remains unapproved; the art test should make both outputs visually understandable.

**Scale:**

- seed: palm-sized asymmetric clamp puck;
- sprout: ankle-high, wedged into a crack;
- juvenile: knee-to-waist-high with two or three anchoring tendrils;
- mature wild plant: spreads 2–3 metres across a wall or pump housing rather than becoming a tree;
- ship cultivar: compact trellis version below waist height.

**Dominant silhouette:** braided root-cables ending in broad gecko-like grip pads. The plant should appear to hold itself against a surface under tension.

**Family anatomy:**

- dark olive or charcoal-green braided tendons;
- pale rubberized undersides;
- flat microhook or suction-scale pads;
- restrained amber compression nodules that brighten slightly only when the plant is under load;
- fibrous seed core protected by two opposing clamp-like lobes;
- no conventional flowers.

**Presentation goal:** production-ready concept sheets and one in-world discovery composition.

**Avoid:**

- Moss Filter honeycomb membranes;
- round Filter Organs;
- broad pale filter leaves;
- cyan-dominant glow;
- ordinary ivy, grapevine, tree roots, mushrooms, tentacle monsters, teeth, mouths, or animal eyes;
- fantasy magic;
- pristine laboratory presentation;
- text-dependent function;
- watermark.

---

## 3. Pass criteria

The experiment passes when:

1. seed, sprout, juvenile, mature wild form, and ship cultivar clearly belong to one species;
2. the plant reads as gripping/anchoring before labels are read;
3. it is visually distinct from Moss Filter at thumbnail size;
4. detachable Grip Pads and harvestable Grip Fiber are obvious;
5. the W002 discovery scene feels like the Dry Cistern rather than Toxic City or a generic cave;
6. shape and material language remain family-friendly, grounded, and buildable;
7. the result provides enough scale and anatomy for a future measured spec if Terry approves it.

---

## 4. Paste-ready Nano Banana prompts

### Prompt A — unified species lifecycle sheet

> Create a clean production concept sheet for one alien plant species called the **Cistern Gripvine**, a Grip-family organism from a wet underground science-fiction world. Show five clearly related forms sharing the exact same anatomy: a palm-sized asymmetric clamp seed puck beside a gloved hand, an ankle-high sprout wedged into a stone crack, a knee-high juvenile, a mature wild plant spreading two to three metres across a vertical wet wall, and a compact below-waist-height ship cultivar growing on a small trellis. The species survives floods and machine vibration by using dark olive braided root-cables that end in broad pale rubberized grip pads with visible microhook or suction-scale texture. Add a few restrained amber compression nodules that brighten only where the plant is actively bearing load. Show detachable Grip Pads and a small bundle of harvestable braided Grip Fiber on the mature form. Every stage must clearly look like the same organism developing and anchoring more strongly. Used-future 1970s science-fiction biology, grounded material realism, child-readable silhouettes, neutral production-sheet background, clear human scale. **Do not use Moss Filter honeycomb membranes, round filter organs, broad pale leaves, cyan-dominant glow, ordinary ivy, flowers, mushrooms, animal eyes, mouths, teeth, fantasy magic, text, or watermark.**

### Prompt B — harvested component sheet

> Create a production prop sheet for the two useful biological outputs of the **Cistern Gripvine**. Show: 1) a palm-sized detachable **Grip Pad**, broad and slightly curved, with a pale rubberized gripping face covered in readable microhook or suction-scale texture and a dark olive fibrous attachment rim; 2) a hand-sized coil of **Grip Fiber**, made from several braided tendon strands, flexible but extremely tough; 3) both components shown beside a gloved VR hand and seated into simple temporary salvage clamps to demonstrate function. Use restrained amber pressure marks only where the Grip Pad is compressed. The components must look biological, harvestable, useful, and clearly related to the same plant. Used-future grounded sci-fi concept art, neutral production background, strong silhouettes. **No honeycomb filtering tissue, no round filter cartridge, no cyan glow, no rope made from ordinary hemp, no tentacles, no corporate logos, no readable text, no watermark.**

### Prompt C — W002 discovery scene

> Create a cinematic but gameplay-readable concept scene of a player discovering a **Cistern Gripvine** in W002, **The Dry Cistern**. The setting is a monumental underground waterworks cathedral: wet black stone arches, deep shafts, rusted pumps, catwalks, an ore-bucket conveyor, crude bolted human machinery attached to much older Architect masonry, and one distant shaft of pale daylight. The Gripvine grows naturally across a slick vertical pump housing beside the player’s route. Its braided dark-olive tendons are visibly under tension, and broad pale grip pads hold against the wet metal while restrained amber compression nodules show where load is carried. One detachable Grip Pad and a short strand of Grip Fiber are visible enough to suggest harvest without making the plant glow like a quest marker. The player should notice it through silhouette, placement, and function, not through arrows or bright emissive effects. Used-future 1970s science-fiction, grounded Prospect-like realism, awe and claustrophobia, playable route composition. **No Toxic City canals, no normal ivy, no Moss Filter honeycomb leaves, no cyan quest glow, no fantasy cave crystals, no text, no watermark.**

### Prompt D — departure/return ship-cultivar pair

> Create a two-panel production concept using the same battered salvage-ship nursery camera, lighting, and two-plot station in both panels. **LEFT — DEPARTURE:** one palm-sized Cistern Gripvine clamp seed puck is seated into an asymmetric keyed socket beside a low metal trellis; the second plot is covered and inactive. **RIGHT — RETURN:** the same plot now contains a compact below-waist-height ship cultivar whose braided tendons wrap the trellis and end in several broad pale Grip Pads, with one small bundle of harvestable Grip Fiber ready to remove. Warm amber practical grow light, worn welded metal, physical knobs and gauges, exposed hoses, cozy used-future ship interior. The plant uses dark olive tendons, pale rubberized gripping surfaces, and only restrained amber load nodules. Preserve the exact environment between panels so growth is the only major change. **No pristine countertop garden, ordinary vegetables, Moss Filter anatomy, cyan-dominant glow, giant wild plant, touchscreen-heavy interface, text, or watermark.**

### Prompt E — optional cross-category consistency test: Grip Clamp

This prompt tests whether the plant grammar can transfer into a salvage-built utility item. It does not approve the device for gameplay.

> Create a production concept sheet for an experimental one-slot belt utility called the **Cistern Grip Clamp**. The compact device combines a battered rectangular salvage-metal tension frame with one replaceable biological Grip Pad harvested from the Cistern Gripvine and a short visible loop of braided Grip Fiber. Its visual function is to anchor a small tool, cable, or repair component temporarily against wet stone or industrial metal. Show front, side, belt-mounted, open-pad replacement, and clamped-to-wet-pipe views. Include one large VR-friendly tension lever, one obvious mechanical locked/unlocked indicator, and a family symbol showing two surfaces being pulled together. The biological pad has a pale rubberized microhook face, dark olive fibrous rim, and restrained amber compression marks under load. Used-future 1970s sci-fi, grounded industrial realism, child-readable silhouette. **No weapon grip, grappling gun, magnet-tether launcher, chest harness, backpack, Moss Filter honeycomb cartridge, cyan-dominant light, modern corporate product styling, readable text, or watermark.**

Run these first:

## 6. Delta-refinement template

After a promising output, use this form:

> Preserve the exact [silhouette/composition/anatomy/palette/views] from the reference. Keep [approved features]. Change only [two or three named failures]. Make [gameplay function] more readable by [specific anatomical or interaction change]. Do not add [likely drift]. Keep the same [camera/sheet/stages/scale]. No text or watermark.

Example:

> Preserve the exact five-stage species sheet, dark olive braided tendons, pale grip pads, amber load nodules, and current proportions. Make every stage share the same two-lobed clamp anatomy from the seed, make the mature form spread laterally across a wall instead of standing like a tree, and make two detachable Grip Pads visually obvious. Do not add honeycomb membranes, flowers, tentacle-monster anatomy, cyan glow, or a second species. Keep the same neutral production-sheet composition and scale references. No text or watermark.
