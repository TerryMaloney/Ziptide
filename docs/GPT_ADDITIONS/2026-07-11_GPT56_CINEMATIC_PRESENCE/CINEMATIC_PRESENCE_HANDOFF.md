## Canonical decision

Ziptide’s visual target is **cinematic presence**, not indiscriminate photorealism:

- close objects receive the strongest inspectable detail;
- world and vista layers simplify in ways that imitate real distance;
- atmosphere, occlusion, motion, scale references and audio create depth;
- lighting uses hierarchy and practical fakes rather than many expensive lights;
- the score is adaptive, sparse and world-specific;
- stable standalone-Quest performance and comfort are part of the visual standard;
- every improvement must be visible through review artifacts and protected by a ratchet after device proof.
