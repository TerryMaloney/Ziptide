*Generalized from Ziptide's locked bible. Each section cites its law (`STORY_GENESIS.md`

---

## 1. Logline *(Law A1 — the fantasy contains the verb)*
One paragraph. The protagonist's JOB must be a playable verb. End with the stakes-question
the whole game answers. *(Ziptide: a contract technician repairs a gate network and learns
it's the wall of a cage.)*

## 2. The locked meta *(Laws A3/A4 — what is actually going on)*
- **The surface spine** (one paragraph — the back-of-the-box truth).
- **The deep layer** (one paragraph — the endgame recontextualization; may live in its own
  protected doc, as `THE_TRANSMISSION.md` does).
- **The recontextualization list:** ≥5 early moments and their before/after meanings.
- Bullet-lock each pillar of the cosmology/world logic (Ziptide pattern: Shell / Architects /
  Observers / Bloom / Pattern / witness — each one resolved sentence).

## 3. Protagonist *(Law A6 + Craft: want-vs-need)*
Who they are · the small mercenary WANT · the thematic NEED · **the HABIT** (one specific,
repeatable behavior the companion can push against) · voice/casting notes · what they never
are (guardrails).

## 4. The companion & the relationship *(Law A6 — the delivery vehicle)*
Arc states (named progression, e.g. Dormant→Integrated) · the relationship RULES (ambient
lines not just plot; a joke, then a real moment, then a joke; memory with its own timeline;
the running bit) · the one moment the companion almost says too much · who talks back and how.

## 5. Factions *(Law A5 — answers to the question)*
First state THE MORAL QUESTION in one sentence. Then the table: Faction · their ANSWER to the
question · want · the player's arc with them · their CONTRADICTION (Craft law) · signature
worlds/beats. Every row must place on the question or be cut.

## 6. Why each mechanic exists *(Law A2 — the unity table)*
Two columns, no empty cells: core mechanic/loop ↔ its in-fiction reason. *(Ziptide: repair =
maintaining the cage; credits = the leash; non-lethal = technician, not soldier.)* This
section is the contract between the story lane and every gameplay lane.

## 7. The revelation ladder *(Law A3 — one notch per chapter)*
Chapter → the one-breath truth-notch → the world range that carries it → the parenthetical
player takeaway. Monotonic; each notch recontextualizes, none contradicts.

## 8. Recurring devices *(Law A7 — slot generators)*
For each device (3–5): name · the per-content-unit SLOT it creates · the payoff mechanism ·
the audit that keeps it honest. *(Ziptide: mystery object/world → Revelation-arc payoff;
wreck logs → the hidden second story; the Signal meter → the world reacting to play.)*

## 9. The endings *(Law A5 — the player's answer)*
N endings, each: name · the answer it gives to the moral question · its final image · what
happens to the companion/key relationships · the flag-math sketch of how it's reached. No two
endings may be palette-swaps of one scene.

## 10. Tone charter *(Law A8 — two audiences)*
The kid's-description and the adult's-description of the same game · content bars (violence/
language/fear ceilings) · what humor sounds like here · what is NEVER done · the emotional
arc of the opening act (feelings first, beats second — Craft law).

## 11. Canon discipline *(Law A9 — the three lists)*
- **LOCKED (date + owner signature):** every §2 pillar and anything else decided.
- **HONORED:** prior canon this bible must not contradict (list it explicitly).
- **DELIBERATELY OPEN:** each open question + who owns closing it + when.
Amendments only through a dated edit to this section. **This section closing = the CANON LOCK
checkpoint** (entry: cohesion audit clean + rubric ≥16/20, no zeros — `STORY_GENESIS.md`
Part D).

## 12. Structure-as-data appendix *(Law A10 — so the pipeline can eat it)*
- **Chapter files:** one per chapter; each world entry headed by the tuple
  `<Biome · hazard · Faction · CompanionState>` (+ ★ marks for signature beats).
- **Per-world template:** every world doc fills the same sections mapping fiction →
  buildable data (role in arc · local premise · REQUIRED mystery object · biome/physics →
  data asset · sky/atmosphere · machines/build · resources · gear found · wreck device ·
  enemies · missions with steps/rewards/completionFlag · map/placement · audio · build
  recipe cross-links). *(Ziptide's `_WORLD_TEMPLATE.md` is the worked example.)*
- **Flags:** named constants, granted-somewhere/consumed-somewhere by construction (the
  flag-graph law).
- **The payoff ledger:** every planted hook → its payoff location → checked at the cohesion
  audit and at every later milestone.
