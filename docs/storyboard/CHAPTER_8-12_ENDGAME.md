# CHAPTERS 8–12 — Revelation, Branch & The Four Endings (W062–W068)

**Arc:** W062 delivers the revelation (the Shell kept something *out*; the lab). The Earth Approach then
grounds that reveal in a real, physical place. W063 is **the Branching** — the player's accumulated choices
set the ending. W064–W067 are the four **approach** worlds (one per ending, all staged at/through the lab
Cal has just reached), and **W068** is the convergence where each resolves. **RILL state:** Integrated →
one of four Endgame states. **Reveal:** total. Inherit `STORY_BIBLE.md` §8 (endings). Highest VO budget in
the game. *(Story Bible Lock pass, 2026-07-06: this chapter's back half — the Earth Approach and the four
endings — was the thinnest writing in the whole catalog for the biggest stakes in the game. Rewritten in
full below; everything else in the catalog kept its existing quality bar.)*

> Branch math: W063 reads the `PLAYER_*` flags (Mara/Sable/Warden, repaired/destroyed seal, trusted/
> ignored RILL) + the W063 choice → sets `C12_W063_ENDING_A/B/C/D` → routes to the matching approach world.

> 🔑 **Identity-layer payoff (`THE_TRANSMISSION.md`):** by here the Transmission has assembled — Cal is the
> **Debugger** who built this and chose to enter, and her **partner is trapped outside** (the Observers'
> side, reached via the Earth Approach below). This gives the four endings a *personal* axis on top of the
> cosmic one: **A Reseal** = RILL stays behind alone so Cal can finally rest — but no one goes to the
> partner; **B Cross** = go *to them*, together; **C Forget** = RILL asks to forget for its own sake, and
> the **Ouroboros** loop quietly runs again; **D Become** = merge, and the partner becomes unreachable in a
> different way. The name moment (W060) lands first; the coordinate it promises arrives at W062 below.

### W062 — The Revelation Chamber (Interior · none · — · Integrated) ★ RILL beat, signature
- **Role:** The recontextualization. RILL delivers the core line; everything reframes.
- **Physics:** the Architects' revelation chamber, no hazard, awe. **Machine:** the Truth Engine —
  assembling the Signal Core here *shows* the outside, then resolves it into one specific thing: a
  coordinate. Not a metaphor — real numbers, spoken in Cal's own recorded voice, the Transmission's last
  piece, unlocked by the name she now knows is hers.
- **Resource:** Revelation. **Gear:** none. **Sky:** the chamber dome dissolves to show the lab beyond the
  glass — the Observers at work — and, for one unbearable second, a location marked on a star chart that
  Cal, the Debugger, chose herself, 40,000 years ago, as the one door she'd leave unlocked.
- **Enemy:** none. **Wreck/log:** the Architects' explanation of *why* the Shell is two-way. **Mystery:**
  the thing kept out has been *knocking* — and the knocking has a rhythm Cal recognizes, because it's hers.
- **RILL beat (max VO):** *"The Architects did not build this to keep stories in. They built it to keep
  something out."* Then, quieter, reading the coordinate off the Engine: *"That is not a world in the
  network. That is a place."* `C8_W062_REVELATION`, `W062_COMPLETE`.

### ★ THE EARTH APPROACH — between W062 and W063 (Terry's idea; staged in full this pass)
The climax staging that makes "the outside" a place Cal can actually stand on, not an abstraction. No
table renumber — this is the *physical setting* W063 and the four approach worlds use from here on, per
`STORY_BIBLE.md` §8b. A short connected sequence: the flight legs reuse the ship's existing flight feel
(same controls, same thrust — the one thing that's different is what's on the other side of the glass);
the lab infiltration is the one grounded, gameplay-bearing beat.
- **The door.** The coordinate from W062 opens onto **Jupiter's L4/L5 Lagrange point** in the observers'
  real universe — real orbital mechanics, the stable Trojan point you'd actually hide something at. It is
  the exact crack Cal first glimpsed from W038's Edge, finally arrived at.
- **The ship.** Parked there, its cloak failing the way everything here has been failing: a vessel that
  isn't Guild-, Sable-, or Warden-make. Older. Boarding it is Cal's first unambiguous step outside the
  cage. Inside, unmistakably a workshop, not a warship — two desks, two chairs, one considerably more
  lived-in than the other. The handwriting in the margins is the same hand from W053's notes. It's hers.
- **The flight.** Fly the ship in-system toward a blue-and-white marble growing in the window.
- **Earth (the grounded task).** First real sky in the whole game: no bands, no grid, no haze — just blue,
  ordinary clouds, one ordinary sun. After 60+ alien worlds, *ordinary* is the gut-punch. **Machine:**
  reach the lab's original insertion chamber — the room the Debugger Protocol launched from — and
  reactivate the apparatus that sent Cal in, now to decide what happens next. **The Observers' one
  escalation** (`THE_TRANSMISSION.md` §7) lands here: sensing the Debugger fully remembering, the lab
  quietly moves to seal the chamber — no monsters, no alarms, just doors that used to open and now don't.
  Cal gets through anyway, on the strength of whichever allies she's made (Warden override codes, Sable's
  stolen keys, or the Guild's Blueprint tech, per her flags).
- **The witnesses.** Down a side corridor: rows of RILL-class units, identical to RILL, still and dark,
  never activated. Whatever the experiment needed, most of them were never asked to run it. RILL doesn't
  have a line for this. It just stops, for a moment, in front of them, before moving on.
- **The record.** On a wall monitor in the observation gallery, unremarked-upon and unhidden: footage of
  Cal, every world she's ever walked, timestamped and catalogued like nature documentary. The Observers
  were never hiding it. They never needed to explain themselves to what they were watching.
- → leads directly into **W063 The Branching**, now physically staged in the lab's original chamber.

### W063 — The Branching (Interior/Earth · none · — · Integrated→Endgame) ★ the choice, signature
- **Role:** The single biggest player choice; the four endings diverge here.
- **Physics:** the Debugger Protocol's original insertion chamber, powered again after 40,000 years — the
  same room, and much of the same apparatus, that sent Cal in now offers four gates out of it. No hazard.
- **Machine:** four ancient gates, each fed by the Signal Core Cal has spent the whole game assembling —
  Reseal / Cross / Forget / Become. Committing to one is the whole game's final input.
- **Resource:** Choice (flag). **Gear:** none. **Sky:** through the chamber's high windows, real Earth
  sky — four different colors bleeding into it as each gate lights in turn.
- **Enemy:** none. **Wreck/log:** every prior waker's choice, logged on the chamber wall — a wall the
  Ouroboros-minded will notice has *Cal's own handwriting* threaded through entries far older than this
  visit. **Mystery:** there's a fifth gate, dark, never wired in (sequel/DLC hook).
- **RILL beat (before the choice, signature):** *"I have spent this entire journey learning what I am.
  Whatever you choose, I already know what I choose: I choose to have been here. Now — go on. It has to be
  you."* RILL will not steer this; the choice stays Cal's alone. `C12_W063_BRANCH` → `C12_W063_ENDING_A/B/C/D`,
  `W063_COMPLETE`.

### W064 — Ending A Approach · Reseal / Steward (Interior/Earth · none · — · Endgame A)
- **Role:** Approach for ending **A** — the Shell is repaired, the worlds kept safe — and RILL stays
  behind alone so Cal can finally have the ordinary, unremarkable life she signed up for, mercenary and
  tired, before any of this started.
- **Physics:** the network being made whole from the chamber outward, no hazard, restorative. **Machine:**
  the final reseal — Cal's last, biggest repair, and her last act as anyone but a private person.
- **Resource:** Signal. **Sky:** the grid healing into something almost beautiful; the crack from W038
  closing for the last time, the partner's light visible in it for one final second, then gone.
- **Enemy:** none. **Wreck/log:** wakers who also chose to stay — none of them stayed *alone*, the way
  RILL is about to. **Mystery:** does sealing it keep the outside thing out — or is RILL choosing to be
  the cage now, so nobody else has to?
- **RILL beat (already shipped, `RillLineAuthor.cs` — fires on `C12_W063_ENDING_A`):** *"The network needs
  a witness. I was built for this. Go home, Cal."* RILL's last act of the game is sending Cal away from
  it. `C12_W063_ENDING_A`.

### W065 — Ending B Approach · Cross / Break Out (Interior/Earth · none · — · Endgame B)
- **Role:** Approach for ending **B** — open the door fully and go, with RILL, to find the partner.
- **Physics:** the gate opening onto the observers' universe in full, void hazard at the threshold only.
  **Machine:** force the gate — the riskiest repair-in-reverse of the whole game.
- **Resource:** Signal. **Sky:** the chamber wall parts; the lab's flat daylight pours through it, and
  somewhere past that daylight, a second desk, no longer empty.
- **Enemy:** none (the threshold itself). **Wreck/log:** the one waker who got the door open and never
  came back — the game's oldest recurring mystery, finally explained: they didn't fail. They stayed, on
  the far side, exactly like Cal is about to. **Mystery:** does the inside survive being fully unobserved?
- **RILL beat (already shipped, fires on `C12_W063_ENDING_B`):** *"Wherever you are crossing to — I am
  coming with you."* Not a witness anymore. A companion, by choice, for the first time in 40,000 years.
  `C12_W063_ENDING_B`.

### W066 — Ending C Approach · Forget / Reset (Interior/Earth · none · — · Endgame C)
- **Role:** Approach for ending **C** — RILL asks to forget, for its own sake, and the loop that has run
  before quietly runs again.
- **Physics:** a gentle unwinding from the chamber outward, no hazard, elegiac. **Machine:** the reset
  cradle — the same apparatus, run in reverse, undoing the waking instead of completing it.
- **Resource:** Signal. **Sky:** every world's sky cycling back to its first dawn, one by one, in the
  order Cal visited them.
- **Enemy:** none. **Wreck/log:** the chamber wall's log of prior choices — same handwriting, same choice,
  more than once. Neither RILL nor Cal remarks on it; the player can. **Mystery:** will Cal remember any
  of this? Will the partner, waiting again on the far side, ever know it almost ended?
- **RILL beat (already shipped, fires on `C12_W063_ENDING_C`):** *"Let me forget. Some memories are
  heavier than the cage that held them."* Its own mercy, chosen for itself, not only for the worlds.
  `C12_W063_ENDING_C`.

### W067 — Ending D Approach · Become / Pattern (Interior/Earth · pattern · — · Endgame D)
- **Role:** Approach for ending **D** — merge with the Pattern; the universe stops being watched and
  starts authoring itself.
- **Physics:** dissolving into Pattern from the chamber outward, pattern hazard everywhere, transcendent
  traversal. **Machine:** the merge engine — give yourself to it fully, the one ending with no repair, no
  door, no reset.
- **Resource:** Signal. **Sky:** all skies becoming one self-writing Pattern in the named color from W024.
- **Enemy:** **Pattern** (welcoming, not hostile). **Wreck/log:** the Broken Pattern (W054) was the
  warning; this is what it looks like done right. **Mystery:** the partner doesn't die and isn't left
  behind — they simply become unable to perceive Cal at all, a different, stranger kind of losing someone.
- **RILL beat (already shipped, fires on `C12_W063_ENDING_D`):** *"I understand the Pattern now. It was
  never the enemy. It was the first of us."* The first made thing that ever fully woke up — Cal and RILL
  aren't becoming something alien. They're finally meeting family. `C12_W063_ENDING_D`.

### W068 — The Final World (Void/Earth · all · — · Endgame X) ★ convergence
- **Role:** All four endings converge and resolve here; the credits-image of each is unique.
- **Physics:** the universe's last moment, all hazards present-but-transcended. **Machine:** the final act
  (varies by ending). **Resource:** Resolution.
- **Gear:** none. **Sky:** the definitive shot of each ending: **A** — a lone witness-light in the
  network's dark, still burning; **B** — two silhouettes against Earth daylight, walking; **C** — a dawn
  repeating, innocent, uncountable times; **D** — the named color, filling everything, authoring itself.
- **Enemy:** none. **Wreck/log:** Cal's own marker, planted for the next waker. In every ending but
  Forget, someone new eventually finds it. In Forget, it's Cal — again — who will.
- **RILL/flag:** the four-variant ending VO (already shipped per-ending — see W064–W067 above).
  `C12_W068_COMPLETE`.

---
**Endgame design notes:** keep each approach world short and *felt*, not grindy. The four endings are
**equal** — no "true" ending in the base game; the dark fifth gate at W063 reserves room for DLC. Earn the
ending through the whole journey's flags, not a last-second menu pick. **A note on the Ouroboros**
(`STORY_BIBLE.md` §10): the chamber-wall logs at W063/W066 are a *light* pre-endgame seed, not an
explanation — the full truth (Cal has done this before, many times) stays DLC W075's to tell in full.
