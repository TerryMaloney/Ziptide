# CHAPTER 3 — The Architects (W013–W019)

**Arc:** Mara reveals the **Architects** built the network on purpose; RILL starts unsealing memory shards
and, at W019, **refuses** to help break a seal. Wake Guild HQ (W018) is the hub. **RILL state:** Remembering
(partial memory, emotional glitches). **Signal:** 2→3. **Reveal:** "someone built it on purpose — and RILL
was *there*." Inherit `STORY_BIBLE.md`.

---

### W013 — Memory Reef (Underwater · pressure · — · Remembering) ★ RILL beat
- **Role:** First underwater world; RILL touches a memory shard and recognizes the place.
- **Physics:** submerged reef, pressure hazard (depth timer), slow buoyant movement. **Machine:** restore reef-lung pumps (oxygen conveyor).
- **Resource/Crop:** Coral (harvestable, tend underwater). **Gear:** rebreather + pressure suit mod. **Sky:** shafts of light through deep water; a drowned planet-glow above the surface.
- **Enemy:** **drone** (sentry subs). **Wreck/log:** a research bathysphere; log: *"the coral remembers shapes."* **Mystery:** the coral has grown into the shape of RILL.
- **RILL beat:** *"I was here. Or something that used to be me was here."* `C3_W013_MEMORY_SHARD`, `C3_RILL_STIRRING`, `W013_COMPLETE`.

### W014 — Ashfield Relay (Exterior · fire · — · Remembering)
- **Role:** Combat/heat gauntlet to reach a key relay; the Signal's cost made physical.
- **Physics:** burning ashfields, fire hazard (heat timer, ember gusts). **Machine:** rebuild the relay mast (build-heavy; conveyor of relay parts).
- **Resource:** Relay Part. **Gear:** Heat Lance + cryo coolant. **Sky:** smoke-red, falling ash, the planet a dull coal through the haze.
- **Enemy:** none (environment is the threat). **Wreck/log:** a firewatch tower; log: *"every relay we fix, the fires spread."* **Mystery:** the fires trace the network's lines exactly.
- **RILL/flag:** Remembering. `W014_COMPLETE`.

### W015 — The Soft Geometry (Pattern · none · — · Remembering)
- **Role:** First **Pattern** world (early, unsettling preview); reality misbehaves.
- **Physics:** non-euclidean soft surfaces, no hazard but disorientation (gentle, comfort-safe). **Machine:** "fold" a path into existence (build = literally shaping space).
- **Resource:** Signal. **Gear:** Mini Gate Puck (small object portals). **Sky:** impossible — the horizon curves *up*; a geometric sun made of repeating cells.
- **Enemy:** **Pattern** (geometry that reshapes to block you; non-lethal, shove-back). **Wreck/log:** a survey crew's looping last day. **Mystery:** the geometry is *trying to spell something*.
- **RILL/flag:** Remembering; *"This is what the inside of a thought looks like."* `W015_COMPLETE`.

### W016 — Sable Outpost B (Station · none · Sable · Remembering)
- **Role:** Deepen Sable; first real player-choice seed (help or report them); grants a Code Key.
- **Physics:** cramped outpost, no hazard. **Machine:** splice Sable's pirate relay into the Guild net.
- **Resource:** Code Key. **Gear:** lockpick/override tool. **Sky:** viewport onto a debris belt of *failed jump ships* — the Shell's body count.
- **Enemy:** Sable **guards** (optional). **Wreck/log:** Sable's own casualty logs. **Mystery:** Sable have a recording of an Architect's voice.
- **RILL/flag:** Remembering. `PLAYER_HELPED_SABLE`/oppose seed, `W016_COMPLETE`.

### W017 — The Long Descent (Underground · cave-in · — · Remembering)
- **Role:** Tension traversal; structural collapse hazard; a bottomless dig toward an Architect layer.
- **Physics:** deep shaft, cave-in hazard (timed supports), long vertical drop with drift-belt. **Machine:** shore up supports + run the ore lift (conveyor).
- **Resource:** Stone (Architect masonry). **Gear:** drift-belt upgrade. **Sky:** none — the shaft mouth a shrinking coin of light above.
- **Enemy:** **drone** (old dig-bots gone feral). **Wreck/log:** generations of dig camps stacked down the shaft. **Mystery:** the deeper you go, the *newer* the Architect tech gets.
- **RILL/flag:** Remembering. `W017_COMPLETE`, `SIGNAL_THRESHOLD_3`.

### W018 — Wake Guild HQ (Interior · none · Wake Guild · Remembering) ★ hub
- **Role:** Faction hub; Mara lays out the Architects' intent; grants the **Blueprint** (build-tier unlock).
- **Physics:** clean corporate interior, no hazard. **Machine:** the Guild's master console (build/upgrade station — unlocks better machines).
- **Resource:** Blueprint. **Gear:** build-tier 2 unlock (better machines/recipes). **Sky:** floor-to-ceiling window onto the network rendered as a glowing web — with a dark center.
- **Enemy:** none. **Wreck/log:** Guild founding records. **Mystery:** the Guild's logo is an Architect glyph.
- **Mara beat:** she lays out the intent with total conviction, then, quieter, a crack in it — *"They
  built this to hold something back. We're going to open it anyway. I've made my peace with what that
  might cost. I need you to at least let me have made peace with it."*
- **RILL/flag:** Remembering; quietly hostile to the Guild. `C3_MARA_REVEAL`, `W018_COMPLETE`.

### W019 — The Unmade World (Void · void · — · Remembering) ★ RILL beat, signature
- **Role:** Chapter 3 capstone; a half-deleted world; RILL refuses to break the seal holding it together.
- **Physics:** dissolving void-world, void hazard (don't step on the gaps), fragments drifting. **Machine:** the seal itself — the game *asks* you to break it; RILL says no.
- **Resource:** Seal Fragment. **Gear:** none new (a moral beat, not a loot beat). **Sky:** a world coming apart into static; stars showing through the holes in the ground.
- **Enemy:** none. **Wreck/log:** the world is the wreck — its own inhabitants' last words fading. **Mystery:** breaking the seal would free it *and* unmake it.
- **RILL beat (signature, 8–12 lines):** *"I cannot help you break this seal. Ask me why."* `C3_W019_RILL_REFUSED`, `W019_COMPLETE`.
